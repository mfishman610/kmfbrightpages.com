Meet the friends

Meet Nova and Bramble

Meet Nova, a curious young unicorn, and her best friend Bramble, a small round hedgehog. In every Unicorn Math Adventures book, the two friends set off on one big story together, and every problem they meet along the way is a math puzzle your child helps them solve.

Each math page is followed by a coloring page that shows the story moving on because your child worked it out. Children don't just practice math; they turn the page to find out what happens next.

One big idea, learned the way children see it

The series is built around number bonds: seeing a number as a whole made of two parts. Nova holds up the whole; Bramble finds the parts. Children count real pictures, fill in simple part-whole diagrams and ten-frames, and find the missing part, with difficulty building step by step through each book.

In every book

  • One continuous story from the first page to the last, with read-aloud lines on every coloring page
  • Short, focused math pages with clear instructions and big, easy-to-count pictures
  • Story coloring pages with bold outlines and large spaces for small hands and crayons
  • One-sided math and coloring pages with blank backs, so markers won't spoil the next activity
  • An answer key for grown-ups, listed by page number
