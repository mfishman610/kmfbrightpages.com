About the book

Today is the big day. Nova, a curious young unicorn, and her best friend Bramble, a small round hedgehog, must get the whole valley ready before the last lantern lights up. Along the way they meet flower invitations to pack, apple pies to bake, festival gems to carry across the stream, a garden to plant, a lantern bridge to light and a few riddles in a cozy hollow. Every problem is a math puzzle, and only your child can solve it.

Look inside

Real pages from the book.

Skills your child practices

  • Counting a group and splitting it into two parts
  • Finding the missing part of a number bond
  • Using ten-frames and counting on
  • Making 10
  • Taking away, and seeing how it connects to adding
  • The swap rule: 2 + 4 and 4 + 2 make the same whole
  • An optional bonus challenge that goes past 10

What’s inside

  • 24 math pages (including 2 optional bonus pages) with short, clear instructions and big, easy-to-count pictures
  • 25 story coloring pages with bold outlines, large spaces and a read-aloud line on each one
  • One continuous adventure from the first page to the grand finale
  • One-sided math and coloring pages with blank backs, so markers won't spoil the next activity
  • An answer key for grown-ups, listed by page number
  • A how-to-use page so you can start together right away
