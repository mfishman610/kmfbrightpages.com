About the book

Every Midsummer, Old Owl rings the great bell of Willowmere at sunset, and the Fair begins. But this morning, the big silver key to the bell tower is gone! A black-and-white feather and an old map send Nova, a curious young unicorn, Bramble, a small round hedgehog, and Old Owl racing the sun: through the forking paths of Hushwood, across a river with a snapped ferry rope, past an old mill, through thick fog and up a hundred stone steps to an old castle. Waiting at the top is a lonely young magpie, and a story about saying sorry and making room for everyone.

Skills your child practices

  • A quick review of making ten to add within 20
  • Splitting a two-digit number into tens and ones, more than one way
  • Adding tens first, then ones
  • Reaching the next ten, then adding across it
  • Finding the missing part by counting up
  • Subtracting within 100 and checking by adding back
  • One bond, four facts
  • Pairs that make 100
  • An optional bonus challenge: hundreds to 1000, and cents to a dollar

What’s inside

  • 25 two-sided math sheets: a front with a story picture and a solved example or guided start, and a back with more practice
  • 26 story coloring pages with bold outlines, large spaces and a read-aloud line on each one
  • One continuous adventure from the first page to the sunset finale
  • Coloring pages printed on one side only, so markers won't spoil the next activity
  • An answer key for grown-ups, listed by page number
  • A how-to-use page so you can start together right away
