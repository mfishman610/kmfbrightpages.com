About the book

Tonight is the Star Shower. Old Owl must drive her little bakery train, the Clover Line, all the way up the mountain to Summit Station, and her best helpers come along: Nova, a curious young unicorn, and Bramble, a small round hedgehog. Old Owl has one rule: fill the front car to ten first! So every stop becomes a math puzzle. They load cookies, apples and sparkling gems, cross the Wobbly Bridge, light their way through the Long Tunnel and meet a friendly bear with a jar of golden honey, and only your child can solve each puzzle along the way.

Skills your child practices

  • Making ten with two ten-frames
  • Teen numbers as ten and some more
  • Finding the missing part of a number bond to 20
  • Adding across ten by making ten first
  • Taking away across ten, two friendly ways
  • Doubles, fact families and jumps on a number line
  • Same or not the same? A see-saw to compare two sums
  • An optional bonus challenge: counting in tens to 100

What’s inside

  • 25 math pages (including 2 optional bonus pages) with short, clear instructions, worked examples and big, easy-to-count pictures
  • 26 story coloring pages with bold outlines, large spaces and a read-aloud line on each one
  • One continuous adventure from the first page to the starry finale
  • One-sided math and coloring pages with blank backs, so markers won't spoil the next activity
  • An answer key for grown-ups, listed by page number
  • A how-to-use page so you can start together right away
